## 1. Executive Summary & Clinical Urgency

Hospitals and clinicians face a dangerous impasse: **they cannot legally send sensitive patient records to cloud-hosted LLMs** due to strict HIPAA and DPDP Act data privacy liabilities. Meanwhile, cognitive fatigue in high-volume clinics causes deadly prescription contraindications to slip through (e.g. ordering NSAIDs for a CKD Stage 3 patient, inducing hemodynamically catastrophic acute kidney collapse).

**MediVault Local** resolves this crisis with a **100% sovereign, air-gapped clinical intelligence workstation**:
- **0 Bytes External Egress:** Operates entirely offline on local hardware with automated socket assertion guards.
- **Deterministic Safety Guarantee:** Clinical flags are generated by a deterministic SQLite pharmacology engine. A local Small Language Model (SLM) provides plain-English explanations, but can **never override deterministic safety gates**.
- **Cryptographic Trust:** Every clinical decision is cryptographically sealed into a local **SHA-256 Merkle ledger** and exported into tamper-evident hospital clearance PDFs.
