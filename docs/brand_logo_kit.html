1. The Outer Shield

Represents the Air-Gap & Safe Harbor Fortress. Built from titanium bevels, symbolizing zero external data egress and compliance under HIPAA § 164.514 and DPDP Act 2023.

2. The Industrial Vault Gear

Represents Impenetrable Local Storage. 8 radial locking lugs with cryptographic keyhole hub, guarding patient health records locally with SHA-256 Merkle chain integrity.

3. The Glowing Medical Cross

Represents Clinical Decision Support. Glowing with bio-luminescent emerald and cyan gradients, emphasizing life-saving patient safety without cloud reliance.
