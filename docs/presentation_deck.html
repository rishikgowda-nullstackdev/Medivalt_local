An 80-year-old grandfather taking blood pressure and water pills visits the ER with back pain. An exhausted doctor in hour 14 prescribes standard Advil (Ibuprofen).

Engineering Depth & Resilience

Four Critical Battles: What Broke & How We Fixed It

BATTLE 1: AI HALLUCINATIONS Patient Hazard

What Broke: The local model (llama3.2:3b) occasionally cleared dangerous drug combos when given noisy or polite user prompts.

How We Fixed It: Built a Deterministic Override Gate. If SQLite finds a medical rule breach, the AI is completely stripped of clearance power and restricted to explanatory notes only.
BATTLE 2: READING PAPER SLIPS OFFLINE Zero-Cloud Privacy

What Broke: Real clinic prescriptions are crumpled paper slips. Cloud OCR (Google Vision, AWS Textract) violates HIPAA's zero-cloud mandate.

How We Fixed It: Embedded RapidOCR (ONNX runtime) to run on local CPU with bounding-box coordinate recovery, followed by 18 HIPAA regex strippers strictly in RAM.
BATTLE 3: DATABASE CONCURRENCY LOCKS Multi-Ward Scale

What Broke: When 6 doctors entered orders at once on a hospital workstation, SQLite crashed with database is locked errors.

How We Fixed It: Enabled SQLite WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) mode with thread-safe connection pooling. Stress-tested with 12 concurrent reviews: 100% success, 0 locks.
BATTLE 4: AIR-GAPPED INTEROPERABILITY Physical Isolation

What Broke: Transferring medical discharge clearances between air-gapped workstations without allowing USB thumb drives (malware risk).

How We Fixed It: Engineered Optical SVG QR Transfer using Base64 + zlib compression. Entire FHIR R4 bundles and Merkle hashes transmit via camera scan.
