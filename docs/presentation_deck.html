Problem Statement & Real-World Impact

The Hospital Trilemma: Regulatory Bans, Cloud Hallucinations & Preventable Deaths

01 / LEGAL IMPASSE HIPAA & DPDP

Cloud AI is Illegal in Clinical Wards

Hospitals cannot transmit Protected Health Information (PHI) to OpenAI, AWS, or Azure without Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) and immense regulatory liability. Even 1 leaked record triggers up to ₹250 Crore / $50,000 per violation penalties.

⚠️ Result: Doctors are forced into manual chart reviews with zero modern AI assistance.
02 / PATIENT MORTALITY Top-5 Death Cause

43% of Polypharmacy Patients at Risk

Adverse Drug Events (ADEs) kill over 100,000 patients annually in North America alone. When multi-morbid patients see 3+ specialists, contraindications like NSAIDs prescribed to a Stage 3 CKD patient slip through fatigued human shifts.

⚡ The "Triple Whammy": ACEi + Diuretic + NSAID triggers sudden acute kidney failure.
03 / STOCHASTIC RISK Black-Box Failure

Pure LLMs Hallucinate Dosages

Generative models hallucinate clearance guidelines, overlook renal eGFR thresholds, or authoritatively approve dangerous drug combos under prompt injection or token drift. A life-critical system cannot gamble on probabilistic text generation.

🛡️ MediVault Solution: Deterministic pharmacology engine overrides all SLM reasoning.
Real-World Scenario: Acute Renal Collapse (CKD + Advil)

Patient on Lisinopril (efferent vasodilation) + Furosemide (hypovolemia) takes OTC Ibuprofen/Advil (afferent vasoconstriction). Glomerular filtration pressure collapses immediately: eGFR plunges from 42 to < 15 mL/min requiring emergency dialysis.

Engineering Depth & Resilience

Four Critical Battles: Problems Faced & Solutions

CHALLENGE 1: LLM HALLUCINATION RISK Clinical Hazard

The Problem: When testing local SLMs (llama3.2:3b), the model occasionally cleared relative contraindications under noisy prompt input, risking patient toxicity.

Our Solution: Built a rigid Deterministic Override Gate. The SLM is strictly prohibited from granting clearance if SQLite flags a rule. The model is confined to explanatory synthesis only.
CHALLENGE 2: ZERO-CLOUD PAPER SLIP OCR Privacy Edge

The Problem: Hospitals ingest physical handwritten/scanned lab slips. Cloud OCR (AWS Textract / Google Vision) violates zero-cloud HIPAA mandates.

Our Solution: Integrated embedded RapidOCR (ONNX runtime) on CPU with bounding-box coordinate recovery, followed by 18 HIPAA Safe Harbor regex strippers entirely in RAM.
CHALLENGE 3: LOCAL DB CONCURRENCY LOCKS Performance

The Problem: Under simultaneous multi-ward prescription entries on a single clinic PC, standard SQLite locked with database is locked errors during audit writes.

Our Solution: Engineered SQLite WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) mode with thread-safe connection pooling. Verified by automated stress tests (12 concurrent reviews across 6 threads with 0 locks).
CHALLENGE 4: AIR-GAPPED INTEROPERABILITY Network Isolation

The Problem: Transferring medical discharge clearances between air-gapped workstations without allowing USB thumb drives (malware vector) or network sockets.

Our Solution: Created Base64 + zlib compressed Optical SVG QR codes. Transmits entire FHIR R4 Bundles and cryptographic Merkle signatures via camera scan alone.
